# 🇮🇳 Maharashtra Darbar — Digital Food & Mess Management Platform

> **Authentic Maharashtrian Food • Good Food • Great People**

Maharashtra Darbar is a modern digital platform designed to bring an authentic Maharashtrian food experience to students, working professionals, families, and food lovers in Ameerpet, Hyderabad.

The platform combines a customer-facing food ordering website with an administrative management dashboard, creating a single digital ecosystem for food discovery, ordering, mess services, and business management.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Finding authentic Maharashtrian food away from home can be difficult, especially for students and working professionals.

**Maharashtra Darbar** provides a digital solution where customers can:

- Explore Maharashtrian dishes and thalis
- View food categories and prices
- Add dishes to a cart
- Place orders through WhatsApp
- Contact the mess directly
- Get directions to the restaurant
- Explore daily mess and tiffin services
- Register/login as a member

At the same time, administrators can manage the operational side of the business through a dedicated dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students and working professionals living away from Maharashtra often struggle to find:

- Authentic Maharashtrian food
- Affordable daily meals
- Reliable mess/tiffin services
- Easy access to food menus and pricing
- A convenient way to place orders
- Centralized management of customers, payments, groceries, and mess operations

Traditional food businesses also face difficulties managing members, payments, groceries, and day-to-day operations efficiently.
